Based on [shadcn/ui/Accordion](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/accordion). An `Accordion` can take a `width`, `gap`, and `dividerWidth` with `AccordionItem` children. Every `AccordionItem` can be animated with `accordionItemRef().open()`, `accordionItemRef().close()`, or `accordionItemRef().toggle()`; with optional durations for all.
